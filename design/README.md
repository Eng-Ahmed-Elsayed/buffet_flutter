# design/

- **`figma/`** holds the current design: PNG exports of the Figma file "DEFI - Kitchen app". What
  was taken from it, remapped and dropped is in
  [docs/figma-redesign.md](../docs/figma-redesign.md).
- **Everything else here is superseded.** `*.dc.html`, `canvas.json`, `buffet-app-screens.html`
  and `_shared.txt` are the earlier `/design` canvas. They are kept for history only; their
  palette and layouts no longer describe the app.
- `logo-defi.png` and `logo-defi-mark.png` are the web's original logo files. The app's own copies
  are in `assets/images/`.
